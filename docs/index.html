au-jobs The Australian labour market, one rectangle per occupation

What this is

A treemap of every ANZSCO 4-digit occupation unit group in Australia. Area is employment (people). Colour is whichever metric you pick above. Built from public ABS and Jobs and Skills Australia data. Source and code: github.com/Jiplet/au-jobs.

What "digital AI exposure" is NOT

  • Not a job-loss or unemployment forecast.
  • Not a model of demand elasticity: a high score can sit next to growing demand.
  • Not a model of regulation, licensing, or professional gatekeeping.
  • Not a model of people's preference for dealing with a human.
  • A rough LLM estimate from a title and major group, not a validated survey.
